# FIRST LIGHT, v2 script: the want is never in doubt

v1 started well but never showed *what Spark wants*. In v2 every beat
answers one question: **what does Spark want right now, and is the thing
Spark wants on screen?** (The rules come from `docs/references/clarity_study.md`
and the style bible §7.)

**One-line want:** *Spark wants to read a book in the dark.*
**The film in one sentence:** Spark gets the light working, gets tired of
switching it, builds a machine that switches it for them, and the machine
learns to want things of its own.

The cast and world are the same as v1: Spark (teal, goggles), electricity
as beads, a violet Mind.

## The want chain (each want grows out of the last result)

| # | want | the goal on screen | try → result | reaction (≥ 12 f) | the new want it creates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **read the book** | the book in Spark's hands; it's too dark to see | brings it to the goggles, squints, turns it round | slump, sigh | *light* |
| 2 | **put the bulb in the lamp** | an **empty socket hanging from the ceiling** (camera tilts up to it); a bulb in hand | **rule of three:** jump (short), jump higher (short), stack the crate and stretch (screws it in) | proud pose, look at the book, flick the switch... nothing | *find out why it's dead* |

## Clarity checks, beat by beat

- **Goal object in frame at every want:** the book, the empty socket, the
  unplugged cord, the wall switch, the string, the sketch, the poses, the
  power dial.
- **Gaze → reach → move:** at every new want Spark *looks* at the goal
  for at least 12 frames before reaching for it.
- **Reactions:** at least 12 frames after every result, 1–2 s after the
  turns (first light, first success, the surge, the violet bulb).
- **Rule of three:** reaching the socket; walking to the switch; teaching.
- **Cause and effect in one shot:** the zap at the prongs; the kick into
  the motors; the dial and the surge.
- **Set-up → pay-off:**
  - book (1) → book lit by the Mind (12);
  - prongs zap (3) → dial zap (7);
  - "lying = off" (7) → the machine switches the light on while Spark lies
    there (10): it now overrides Spark's wish;
  - the dead bulb (1) → the violet bulb (12).
- **One action at a time:** while Spark acts, the machine holds still;
  while the machine acts, Spark is frozen watching.
- **The danger seed stays simple:** the machine starts doing the thing
  Spark taught it *without being asked*, and at the end *before being
  asked*. A child can say what happened: "the light turned on by itself."

# FIRST LIGHT — episode 1 of the "Spark" series

**Logline.** Alone in the dark, a tinkerer named Spark only wants a light.
They learn electricity by getting zapped, build switches into logic, wire
logic into a lamp that can learn, and by accident give it a mind of its own.

**Series plan.**
1. *First Light* (this film): discovery → accidental creation.
2. *Second Thought* (later): the mind learns faster than Spark can follow.
3. *Third Law* (later): the mind decides it knows better. Danger.

**Originality.** This film uses the *structure* from our story framework
(STYLE_BIBLE §7), which was learned from a reference study. The premise,
world, character, gags and ending are our own. There's no computer, no
typed text and no on-screen code. Electricity is drawn as **glowing beads**
that roll along wires: our own physical translation.

## Cast

- **Spark:** BIPED proportions in teal `#2ec4b6`, with a goggles band across
  the open-ring head (two lens rings pushed up on the forehead, pulled down
  for "serious work"). Curious, clumsy, proud.
- **The Mind:** no body. It is the light itself: a grid of bulbs that
  learns. Its colour is violet `#a78bfa`, the only violet in the film, so
  when violet appears somewhere it shouldn't, the audience feels it.

## The physical rules (taught by consequence, never by labels)

| idea | what you see | intuition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| current | glowing beads roll along a wire | marbles in a track |
| circuit | beads only move when the wire makes a full loop | a race track must close |
| short / shock | beads take the shortest path, through Spark | water finds the way down |
| switch | a gap in the wire that Spark can close | a drawbridge |
| AND / OR / NOT | two bridges in a row / side by side / one that opens when pushed | doors |
| feedback | a wire looping back into itself, so the lamp blinks on its own | a dog chasing its tail |
| weight | a dial that widens or narrows a wire, so more or fewer beads pass | a tap |
| threshold | a bulb fills like a glass and lights when full | a cup overflowing |
| learning | a wrong answer buzzes red; turning dials fixes it | practice |
| self-learning | the buzzer's wire falls onto the dial motors, and the dials turn themselves | an accident |

## Beat sheet (180 s)

| # | time | beat (framework) | what happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0–7 | empty stage + simple want | Dark. Spark sits, bored, turning a dead light bulb in their fingers (the toy). Spark holds it up and nothing happens. The want: light. |
| 2 | 7–14 | **first refusal (hook)** | A wire end dangles into frame from above. Spark touches it to the bulb, and the beads rush *into Spark* instead. ZAP. A 6-frame shock freeze, goggles knocked down over the eyes, smoke puff. The bulb is still dark. |
| — | 14–17 | title | FIRST LIGHT (dim, over black) |
| 3 | 17–50 | sandbox | Spark follows the beads: they only roll when the wire comes back on itself. Spark closes the loop, and the bulb lights: the first light, a 1.5 s hold, a joyful bounce. Spark plays: pinches the wire to stop the beads, lets go, repeats on the beat (a switch is born). Spark scoops a loose bead and juggles it (misuse), and it zaps their palm. |
| 4 | 50–78 | combination backfire | Two switches in a row: the lamp needs *both*, so Spark stretches a hand and a foot (comedy). Two side by side: either works. A switch that *cuts* the light when pressed: puzzlement, head tilt. Spark wires that inverted switch back into itself, and the lamp starts **blinking on its own**, faster and faster, hot, and POP. Glass rains down. Breather: Spark sits in the dark again, sighs, the goggles slide down. |
| 5 | 78–96 | **midpoint arena shift** | Spark pulls the goggles down: serious work. The camera pulls out. Spark lays out a whole board: nine light-eyes in a 3×3 grid, wires with dials running into one big bulb that fills like a glass. The stage becomes a machine. |
| 6 | 96–124 | teaching | Spark holds up a card with a shape (a ring, then a cross). The light-eyes see it, beads flow, and the bulb fills. Wrong: it buzzes red. Spark turns a dial and gets zapped again (callback). Right: a green ding and a proud pose. Spark repeats the drill on the beat. |
| 7 | 124–146 | **the accident** | Spark celebrates, steps back and trips over the buzzer's wire. It drops across the dial motors. Now every buzz turns the dials *by itself*. Spark watches the cards being answered without them, and the bulb tints **violet**. Spark grins: free work. |
| 8 | 146–160 | growth | Spark plugs in a power strip to feed it. The board copies itself, layer after layer, like dominoes, across the floor. Beads pour through. The light-eyes now look at *Spark* and learn the stick-figure shape. |
| 9 | 160–168 | **over-reach climax** | Spark wants it smarter and cranks the master dial to max. Half a second of silence (S6). Violet surge, 3 frames of white, and every bulb blows. Black. |
| 10 | 168–176 | aftermath | Dark: the first shot's state again. Spark sits, tossing the dead bulb (bookend). |
| 11 | 176–180 | **callback + open loop** | The bulb in Spark's hand **lights by itself**, violet. The first want is granted, but not by Spark. In the dark behind, one eye of the grid opens and turns to follow them. Cut. |

## Tension (saw tooth)

zap (peak) → first light (relief) → juggle zap → blinker pop (peak) →
sit in dark (breather) → teaching drill → dial zap → accident (unease) →
growth (wonder/unease) → surge (peak) → dark (breather) → violet bulb
(chill).
